N°03 — HERITAGE

Since “1987”

MONZA → MILANO

We don’t build bicycles.
We build Italian history.

Ceste began in a two-bay garage in Monza, where our founder welded steel frames for local racers after his shift at the factory. Thirty-nine years later, every Ceste is still touched by human hands in our Milano atelier — laid, filed, painted and signed by one builder, one bike at a time. No mass production. No compromises. Only the ride.

N°04 — METHOD

The “Craft”

THREE STEPS. NO SHORTCUTS.
(FIG. 03 — LUG DETAIL, FILED BY HAND)
  • 01

    The Frame — “Telaio”

    Aerospace-grade carbon or Columbus steel, laid and welded by hand. Each tube is measured twice, cut once, and numbered forever.

  • 02

    The Paint — “Vernice”

    Fourteen layers of lacquer, hand-polished to a mirror. Our bianco Assoluto white is mixed in-house and matched to no one.

  • 03

    The Build — “Montaggio”

    One master mechanic assembles your bike from first bolt to last, torque-checks every interface, and test-rides it on the streets of Milano.
