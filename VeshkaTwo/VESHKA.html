Built to “Last”

Veshka started in 2021 with a single rail of twelve white tees and one rule: nothing disposable. Every drop is cut in limited runs from traceable European mills — organic cotton, undyed wool, recycled canvas — and finished by hand in our atelier.

No shouting logos. No seasonal landfill. Just a monochrome system of pieces that layer, match and last.
